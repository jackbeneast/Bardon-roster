Start here

The five minutes that make everything else make sense.

The rules the hub runs on

  • Nothing reaches a client without you. Every quote, invoice and text opens as a draft for you to check and send. Two exceptions, both off until you switch them on: the instant "got it" text to new leads (Growth hub) and the arrival and finished texts clients can opt in to (Desk → Arrival & finish texts).
  • One job, one place. A job on the roster carries everything: team, shifts, areas, checklists, photos, notes, the client link, the agent's view and the money.
  • Multi-day jobs are one job. Add days to a job rather than making new jobs. Clients and agents see one job and one report.
  • Texts go from the business number (Mobile Message) and every conversation lives in Messages, never your own phone.
  • Xero stays the official record for GST and BAS. The hub feeds it payments and expenses.

How do I…

The common jobs, start to finish.

A new lead came in through the website
  1. You get a ping. Open Pipeline → New (or Today → Leads and people waiting).
  2. Read the request: service, property, timing, access and photos.
  3. Tap Quote it. The quote opens with their details filled in.
  4. Set bedrooms and bathrooms. The three packages build themselves. Adjust, add extras, then Preview and Send.
  5. Need more info first? Tap Text on the card. The "first reply" template opens in Messages.
The Growth hub tracks how fast you reply to new leads, and pings you if one has been waiting past your reply target (7am to 7pm).
Someone rang for a quote
  1. Pipeline → New → New quote for a phone enquiry (or + Add → Quote).
  2. Fill in client, property, service, beds and baths. Send as normal.
Book a pre-sale clean from an accepted quote
  1. Open the quote (ping, Today, or Money → Quotes).
  2. If they accepted online, it's marked accepted and, if they picked a date, it's already on the roster. If they told you instead, tap their package choice, then Mark accepted.
  3. Approved? Turn into a job: date, team arrival and finish time → Add to roster.
  4. Under Next steps, Send the deposit invoice.
  5. Send the Booking confirmation text. It links them to their job page.
  6. On the roster, open the job: assign the team, check the areas, set the agent if there is one.
Finish a job properly
  1. Every area done with after photos, lock-up ticked.
  2. Finish tab → mark the job ready → Clock off now.
  3. Best before & afters: pick the pair for each area, or Skip, none. If you skip, nothing tells the client to expect them.
  4. Invoice the balance (the deposit already paid is shown and taken off) and send it. The thank-you and job link go with it.
  5. Upload the walkthrough video and remaining photos if you have them.
  6. At 6pm, log actual hours and costs when the ping arrives.
Add a new cleaner
  1. If they applied through the careers page: Pipeline → Hiring → open them → score, notes → Book trial and add to the team list.
  2. Didn't apply? Tools → Onboarding new starters → + Add a new starter.
  3. In Onboarding, send their contract and new starter form (each from the business number).
  4. Tools → Team passcodes → create their passcode → Text it.
  5. Tools → Manage team → Send invite to the team app.
  6. Put their first shift on the roster. First-day info shows on their roster before the day.
  7. When everything is ticked: All set up: move to On the team.
Run the weekly pay
  1. Tools → Run pay (or Team & pay → Run).
  2. Check each person's hours. They come from clock-in and clock-off, otherwise the roster. + Add shift for anything missing.
  3. Set the payment date and save. Payslips are made.
  4. Pay the net amounts from the bank, then mark sent. Mark super and PAYG paid when you pay them.
Payroll is also set up in Xero. Keep the two matching until you settle on one.
A client wants extra work done mid-job
  1. Desk → Bookings → the job → Extras for their OK.
  2. What it is, price inc GST, an optional note → Send for their OK.
  3. They approve or decline on their link. Approved extras go onto the unpaid invoice for that booking, if there is one.
Something got damaged
  1. Desk → Records → Incidents & damage → Log an incident.
  2. Date, job, what happened, who was on, what you did, photos.
  3. Print or PDF to send your insurer. Tick reported to insurer.
Stop servicing someone

Desk → Records → Do not service → Add someone. If they fill in the quote form, you get a warning and the lead is flagged. Nothing is sent to them.

Quotes & invoices

Branded quotes and invoices, a client link and a PDF, paid by bank transfer or card.

The Money home screen
Tools → Quotes & invoices

Owed to you and Overdue at the top, then Quotes and Invoices with their status: draft, sent, opened, accepted, paid, overdue. Settings live at the bottom: saved items and scope, package options and hours, business, bank and numbering, and Import from Jobber.

Quote requestsYour own request form, not Jobber's
Tools → Quote requests

Clients fill in bardon-roster.netlify.app/quote: service, property, timing, access, photos, contact. Each request is here with everything they sent. Open one and tap to quote it. Jobber requests can still be added by hand with + Add a request from Jobber.

Build a quotePackages, add-ons, price by area, price check
+ Add → Quote, or Quote it from a request
  1. Client: pick a previous client, a roster job, or type new. Mobile and email let you send it.
  2. The job: property, suburb, service date (leave blank to let them pick), service, bedrooms, bathrooms.
  3. Fill in price and scope. For pre-sale and deep cleans, three packages build from beds and baths: all-inclusive down to a budget version.
  4. Add-ons they can tick. Anything a package already covers is hidden for that package. Add-ons with no price show as "priced to your home" and come to you as a request.
  5. Optional: + From saved items, + Price by area (kitchen, bathroom, toilet, laundry, living area) for clients who want less, or + Blank item.
  6. Details: valid until, deposit to book, note to client, include service policies.
  7. Price check (optional): enter staff hours, your hours and materials to see break-even and the price that pays you and grows the business.
Changed a package price by hand? Changing beds or baths won't overwrite it until you tap Rebuild from beds and baths.
Your hourly rate for package maths lives in Package options. Clients never see it.
Send a quote or invoice
  1. Open it → Preview to see what the client sees.
  2. Send: choose Text (from the business number, lands in Messages) or email (Open Mail), or copy the message to paste anywhere.
  3. Edit the wording for this one send if you like. Templates are under Messages → Templates.

Once sent, changes show on the client's link straight away. The Progress panel shows created, sent, opened by client and paid. Text a reminder when it's sitting.

Accepted → on the roster

Clients accept online and pick their package (and their date, if you left it blank). If they tell you instead, tap their choice, then Mark accepted.

  1. Approved? Turn into a job: date, team arrives, finishes around → Add to roster. Client, address and service are filled in.
  2. Booking confirmation: check the text and send. It links them to their job page.
  3. Assign the team on the roster.
Clients pick their date (Money settings): set jobs per day, earliest day and weeks ahead. Accepted quotes with no date go straight onto the roster on the day they choose.
Deposits50% to secure, due the same day

After acceptance, the quote's Next steps show Send the deposit invoice. Pre-sale, deep and bond deposits are due on the day; the booking stays tentative until it's paid. Today and the 4pm ping show any still unpaid.

Balance and full invoices

From the quote: Invoice the balance after the clean, or Invoice in full if there was no deposit. The invoice shows the full amount, the deposit already paid, and what's left.

Not from a quote? + Add → Invoice. Use Already paid → + Add a payment already made for money paid before this invoice. It isn't counted as new money in your books or Xero.

Link the invoice to the roster job and the job report goes with it.

Record a payment, tips and receipts
  • Card payments through Stripe record themselves.
  • Bank transfer, cash or other: open the invoice → Record payment → amount, date, how they paid.
  • Paid more than the invoice? Tick Tip included or use Add tip. Tips are kept apart from the invoice, the receipt thanks them, and Xero files them separately.
  • Send a receipt from the invoice. View receipt to save it as a PDF.
Regular cleans: Invoice now

Finished regular cleans from today and yesterday that aren't invoiced show on Today. Invoice now works out hours × your hourly rate + GST, using hours you logged, then clock-in and clock-off, then the roster.

Settings worth setting once
  • Saved items, scope and prices: reusable lines, beds/baths per-visit prices, service policies shown at the end of quotes.
  • Package options and hours: names, base hours, hours per bed and bath, highlights, full scope, which extras each package covers, your hourly rate.
  • Business, bank and numbering: details on every document, bank details, card payments, numbering and defaults, Clients pick their date.
  • Import from Jobber: bring across old quotes and invoices.

The roster

Who's where, when, and whether they've confirmed.

Reading the roster
Tab bar → Roster

Calendar view by week, days colour-coded. Multi-day jobs show on each of their days. Tap any job to open it. Filter by person to see one cleaner's week.

Add or edit a job
+ Add → Job on the roster, or tap a job → Edit
  1. Client name, street address, suburb, service.
  2. When and who: date, start, finish and the people on it. Someone starting late or leaving early? Set their own shift times.
  3. + Add another day copies the last day to the next one. Each day shows on the roster and the team confirms each.
  4. Agent and Ready for (photography, open home, settlement, bond inspection) if it's an agent job.
  5. Areas to clean: remove what isn't there, add what is. Every home is different.
  6. Job price, meeting spot, notes for the team → Save job.
The same client booked again on another job? The sheet offers Make it one booking so it's one job and one report.

From an open job: Job updates (the on-the-day screen), Client view, Text client, Text team, Duplicate, Remove day.

Shift confirmations

The team confirms each shift with one tap on the roster or in the team app (I'll be there or Can't, with a reason). You get a ping either way, and at 5pm you get a list of tomorrow's shifts not confirmed and jobs with nobody on them.

Tools → Shift confirmations shows everything over the next two weeks. Mark one by hand if they replied by text. Changing someone's times resets their confirmation.

Text the team, copy the roster
  • Text team (from a job): each person gets their own text from the business number. Alarm and lockbox codes are fine here.
  • Copy roster to text (Tools): pick everyone or one person, copy the week and paste it anywhere. It asks them to confirm on the roster.
First-day infoWhat new starters see before shift one

Tools → First-day info. New starters see it on the roster before their first shift with the date, start time and address filled in from their job. Tick "New starter" against them in Manage team. You get a 5pm ping if they haven't read it.

Regular clients
Tools → Regular clients

+ Add a regular client: name, mobile, address, how often, next visit, times, team, notes. The hub keeps 8 weeks of visits on the roster. Saving updates future visits nobody has confirmed or started yet. Stop this regular client when they finish.

Regulars still in Jobber show under "From Jobber, not set up here yet". Check them, untick any that have stopped, Bring these over.

Manage team and agents
  • Manage team (Tools → Roster): add people, mobiles, new-starter flag, Photos for clients (only shown once you tick they're OK with it), and team app invites.
  • Real estate agents (Tools → Clients): add the agent and agency. Each gets a private link showing only the jobs you tag with their name. Text it once. New link if it ever goes to the wrong person.
Wage estimate

Tools → Wage estimate: gross wages and total cost (with super) for this pay period, from the roster. Your own shifts aren't counted. Pay settings: weekly or fortnightly, a period start date, the 30-minute unpaid lunch on shifts over 4½ hours, and extra public holidays (Queensland's for 2026 and 2027 are in).

Restock list

When the team flags a product as running low on a job, it lands on Tools → Restock list. Only you see it. Tap Bought once you have it.

On the day

The job screen: Areas, Photos, Team, Notes, Finish. Everything here feeds the client's link and report.

Open the job screen

Today → tap the job (or Roster → job → Job updates). Five tabs along the top. Areas, checklists, photos and notes carry across every day of a multi-day job. Clock in and off is per day.

Client view shows exactly what they see. Your own visits don't count in their engagement.

Areas tabBefore photos, checklist, after photos
  1. Tap an area. Take before photos.
  2. Work the checklist. Tick all, then untick what you didn't do. Unticked items don't show on the client's report.
  3. Take after photos. At least one after photo is needed before an area can be marked done.

+ Add or remove areas if the home differs from the booking. Access details the client left are at the top under "From the client: access".

Photos tabQuick shots, bulk upload and sorting
  • Quick photo: choose Before or After, tap the area, the camera opens.
  • Lots from your photo library: upload them all, then file them later under To sort.
  • Sorting: tap a photo to see it big and file it. Tap the circles to pick several, or pick the first and last of a run and Select between. Choose before or after, then the area.

Filed photos show on the client's link under that area.

Walkthrough videosThe property tour

On the job screen, Add walkthrough video. Long videos are fine. Add several clips and they play in order. Clients and agents see it as a property tour once it's processed.

Keep the screen open until the upload finishes. If the signal drops it carries on where it left off. If one says "Didn't process", delete and upload again.
Team tabClock in and off, days

Each person taps I've arrived and Clock off. Hours come from these taps, so clock off when you leave the house. Forgot? Type in times. + Add another day to this job lives here too.

You get pinged if someone hasn't checked in 15 minutes after their start, or hasn't clocked off 90 minutes after their end.

Notes tabFor the agent, and supplies
  • Notes for the agent: anything they should know before photos or buyers, like a cracked oven seal. The agent sees it under "For your attention".
  • Running low on supplies? Only you see these. They go on the restock list.
Finish tab and Job readyLock-up, finish time, next steps
  1. Check every area is done, afters are taken and the house is locked up. Tick the lock-up items.
  2. Finish time the agent sees: update the estimated finish if it moves.
  3. Mark the job ready, then Clock off now.
  4. Job ready · next steps opens:
    • Best before & afters: pick a pair per area, Skip, none, or Decide later.
    • Invoice the balance, or send the balance invoice already drafted. The thank-you and job link go with it.
    • Or just Send the thank-you & job link. You check the text before it goes.
Once someone has arrived, the job shows Tell the client we've started: one tap opens the "we're on site" text with their job link.
Check for lost progress

Something missing after a job was edited, moved or merged? Job screen → Check this job for lost progress. It finds photos and ticks saved under areas the job no longer lists, under other jobs for the same client or street, and under jobs no longer on the roster. Tick what belongs and Put it back.

Client & agent links

What people outside the business see. No logins for them.

The client's job link

Every job gets a private link (it goes in the booking confirmation and thank-you texts). It shows:

  • Booking details, add to calendar, text Jack.
  • How do we get in? They fill in keys, parking, pets and notes for the team. (They're told not to put alarm or lockbox codes here.)
  • Before we arrive and What happens next.
  • Live progress while you're cleaning, with the house illustration filling in area by area, the finish estimate, and your feed updates.
  • Before & afters first, then every area with its ticked checklist and photos, the property tour, notes for their attention, and lock-up.
  • Who's looking after the home (first names and photos you've cleared).
  • How did we go? Really happy → Google review; Something's not right → straight to you. Then Book again.

Their quote and invoices open from the same place. They can share a view-only copy with someone else.

The agent portal

Each agent's private link shows only their tagged jobs: On site today, Coming up, Handover reports, Price & billing (every quote and invoice for their jobs), your insurance details and a certificate of currency request, Book a clean, and Message Jack (lands in your Messages; your replies reach them by text).

From a job they can Share with the vendor: the vendor gets that clean only.

Agent bookings land in Pipeline → New as booking requests.

Bond evidence pack

For bond cleans, a printable pack: the clean, the entry condition report, area by area with photos, notes from the team, and lock-up. Open it from the client link and print or save as PDF for the tenant or agency.

Profit & books

What you actually make, per job and per hour.

Profit & expenses
Tools → Profit & expenses

Profit per hour by service, the coming 28 days, your plan (break-even and goal), profit and loss, GST for BAS, cash in, profit by job and the last six months. "What to do" at the top lists anything missing: unpriced jobs, unlogged hours, expenses not logged for a while.

Revenue counts on each job's first day. Wages use actual hours where logged, otherwise rostered hours. Your own hours aren't costed: they show as your $/hr.
Log an expenseSnap the receipt, 10 seconds
+ Add → Expense
  1. Pick up to a handful of receipt photos at once. Claude reads them.
  2. Check the amount, GST and category, then save.
  3. No receipt? + Manual.

Regular outgoings: add everything you pay on repeat (insurance, phone, software, fuel) once and it's counted automatically. Go through the list and tap Don't pay on what doesn't apply.

Log actual hours and costs per job

At 6pm you get a ping for today's finished jobs. Open each and log the real total hours and any costs. That turns into the job's real hourly rate and profit, and feeds the Pricing check on the desk.

Xero

Every payment you receive and every expense you log goes to Xero as Receive Money and Spend Money (receipt attached), with GST split out. Nothing goes across as an invoice or bill, so Ignite's invoice and bill limits don't bite. It syncs every 15 minutes. Match them to your bank feed in Xero's reconcile screen.

Setup (once, on a laptop) is step by step on the Books page under Xero for BAS. Set GST to the cash basis in Xero.

Jobber sync

While regulars are still in Jobber: paste Jobber's calendar link (Settings → Calendar sync) under Books → Jobber sync. Recurring visits come onto the roster every 6 hours and keep their times in step. One-off Jobber jobs are listed for you to add or ignore.

Price calculator
Tools → Price calculator

Service, beds, baths, cleaners and hours, your hours, travel and costs. It shows break-even, your pay after tax, what's kept to grow, the client price inc GST and the 50% deposit, then Start a quote. "How past jobs went" compares with real logged jobs. Set your targets and overheads once.

Employment contracts

Team profiles

The new starter form fills each profile: contact, tax, super, bank, ID, availability and paperwork ticks. Private details are hidden until you tap Show in full.

HELP repayments aren't worked out automatically yet. Use the tax override on their pay.
Team passcodes

Safety nets

Backups

The hub backs itself up every night. Desk → Records → Backups: Back up now before anything big, and Download everything every month or so into Google Drive, so there's a copy outside Netlify.

Privacy and access
  • Hub pages are hidden from search engines.
  • Client, agent, vendor and pay links are private, unguessable links. Replace any that goes to the wrong person.

Quick fixes

A page says "Log in first"

Open the hub home, log in with your password, then go back to the page. Each device and the home-screen app need their own login.

Something looks out of date

Tap Refresh at the top. If you've just deployed a change, close and reopen the home-screen app.

A text didn't send

Check Messages for the "Mobile Message isn't connected" banner, and that the number didn't reply STOP. While it isn't connected, texts open on your own phone instead of going from the business number.

Photos or ticks went missing

Job screen → Check this job for lost progress. Also check To sort on the Photos tab.

A cleaner can't log in

Tools → Team passcodes: check they have a mobile on file and a passcode set; Unlock if locked, or give them a new one.

Someone's hours look wrong on pay

Hours come from clock-in and clock-off where used, otherwise the roster, minus the unpaid lunch on long shifts. Fix the times on the job's Team tab or in the pay run before saving.

Xero sync shows a problem

Books → Xero: anything that "didn't go across" is listed. Reload accounts from Xero and check the account mappings, then save.
